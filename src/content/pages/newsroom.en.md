The po ludzku newsroom has closed. Its records for neurodivergent adults, people with disabilities and caregivers are no longer updated. Do not treat old addresses as current information about the law.

poludzku.info now hosts the GitHub task console. It shows one task, whose move it is and what comes next.

## Where to look instead

Check rules and deadlines in official sources.

- [gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl): the public administration portal.
- [ZUS](https://www.zus.pl): contributions, benefits and pensions.
- [PFRON](https://www.pfron.org.pl): support for people with disabilities.
- [Commissioner for Human Rights](https://bip.brpo.gov.pl): help when an office breaks your rights.
- [Fundacja Serio](https://serio.org.pl): the publisher of this site.
- [krok](https://krok.cloud): one task, one step.
